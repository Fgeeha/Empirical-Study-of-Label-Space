All models are initialized from ImageNet-pretrained weights; the classification heads are replaced and trained from scratch for the shared class set. Training uses the AdamW optimizer~\cite{loshchilov2019adamw} with a learning rate of ${3e-04}$, weight decay ${0.0001}$, and a batch size of 32. Baseline models are trained for up to 30 epochs with early stopping (patience 5). Training-time data augmentation consists of random horizontal flipping, random rotation up to $\pm15^\circ$, color jitter (brightness=0.2, contrast=0.2, saturation=0.2); validation and test images receive only resizing to $224 \times 224$ and ImageNet-channel normalization. For fine-tuning on the target domain, the learning rate is reduced by a factor of 0.1 to ${3e-05}$, the backbone is frozen for the first 3 epochs, and early stopping with the same patience is applied.
